Hint: The -A flag is "everything on":
  - Version detection
  - OS detection
  - Default scripts
  - Traceroute

Critically, it also scans ALL 65535 ports — not just the top 1000.
This is why it takes longer, but reveals hidden services.

  sudo nmap -A 10.0.2.5

Look for the MySQL port (3306) and its version string.
