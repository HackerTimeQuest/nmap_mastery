Hint: The firewall on the target drops packets destined for ports
above 1000. Two evasion techniques work here:

Fragmentation (-f) splits each IP packet into tiny fragments.
Simple firewalls that only inspect complete packets will let them through.

  sudo nmap -f -p 9090 10.0.2.5

Decoys (-D) inject fake source IPs into the scan. The target
sees connections from random IPs, hiding you in the noise.

  sudo nmap -D RND:10 -p 9090 10.0.2.5

Combine them:
  sudo nmap -f -D RND:10 -p 9090 10.0.2.5

When port 9090 shows as open, connect with nc to grab the flag.
